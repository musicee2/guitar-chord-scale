Privacy Policy / 개인정보 처리방침

Effective: 2026-11-13 · 시행일: 2026-11-13

English

“Guitar Chord & Scale Roadmap” does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal data.

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads, analytics, third-party SDKs or tracking.
  • Settings (theme, language, tone, volume, metronome tempo and volume), starred fingerings, quiz results, practice days and review items are stored on your device only and are removed when you delete the app.
  • If you tap the download icon on a chord or scale diagram and choose “Save to Photos”, the app asks for permission to add that image to your Photos. The app only adds the image you chose; it does not read your photo library.
  • The app works offline and does not connect to any server.

Contact: musicee2@gmail.com
